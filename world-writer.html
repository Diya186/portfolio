Writing is
how I think.

Before I was a coder or a designer or a student, I was a reader. Words were the first technology I ever loved — the way you can arrange twenty-six letters into something that makes a stranger feel less alone.

My writing lives at the intersection of personal and precise. Whether it's an essay or a poem at midnight, I try to say exactly what I mean. No filler. No performance. Just the honest version.

Words on paper. Words out loud. Same discipline. I was school media lead, volleyball team captain, and a public speaking competitor — and every version of that was just finding a different format for the same thing.
